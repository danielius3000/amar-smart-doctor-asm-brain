# Project Charter v1.0

**Project:** Amar Smart Doctor

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Approved

**Owner:** CEO & CTO

---

# Purpose

This Project Charter defines the long-term purpose, scope, boundaries, success criteria, and strategic commitments of Amar Smart Doctor.

It serves as the highest-level reference for determining whether future initiatives align with the organization's mission.

When uncertainty arises, this document takes precedence over feature requests or short-term opportunities.

---

# Vision

Build the world's most trusted AI-native healthcare knowledge platform by transforming verified medical evidence into structured, governed, and reusable knowledge.

---

# Mission

Empower patients, healthcare professionals, researchers, and future AI systems with trustworthy healthcare knowledge that is accurate, traceable, continuously improving, and accessible.

---

# Strategic Identity

Amar Smart Doctor is an AI-native Healthcare Knowledge Platform.

We are not a traditional content website.

We are not a hospital information system.

We are not an Electronic Medical Record (EMR) platform.

We are not an AI diagnosis system.

Our primary product is governed healthcare knowledge.

Every digital product we create exists to deliver that knowledge.

---

# In Scope

The organization will focus on:

- Healthcare knowledge engineering
- Medical evidence collection
- Knowledge verification
- Knowledge structuring
- Medical review workflows
- Doctor discovery
- Disease information
- Treatment information
- Preventive healthcare education
- AI-assisted knowledge production
- Knowledge-driven digital products
- Search and discovery
- Healthcare education
- Continuous knowledge improvement

---

# Out of Scope

The organization will not operate as:

- Electronic Medical Record (EMR)
- Hospital Management System
- Clinic Management System
- Direct telemedicine platform
- Personalized diagnosis engine
- Emergency medical service
- Medical prescription provider
- Medical insurance provider
- Pharmaceutical manufacturer

Future expansion into these areas requires an explicit strategic decision.

---

# Strategic Assets

The organization recognizes the following assets in order of strategic importance:

1. Verified, Structured, and Governed Knowledge
2. Organizational Trust
3. Medical Review System
4. Knowledge Engineering Methodology
5. ASM Brain
6. AI Workforce
7. Engineering Platform
8. Products
9. Brand

---

# Success Criteria

The long-term success of Amar Smart Doctor will be measured by:

- Trustworthiness
- Medical accuracy
- Knowledge quality
- Knowledge coverage
- Knowledge freshness
- User value
- Platform scalability
- Operational sustainability
- Responsible AI usage

Traffic alone is not considered success.

---

# Strategic Commitments

The organization commits to:

- Evidence before opinion.
- Human governance over AI autonomy.
- Continuous learning.
- Transparent knowledge updates.
- Responsible AI adoption.
- Long-term thinking.
- Platform-first architecture.
- Single Source of Truth.
- Continuous improvement.

---

# Decision Principles

Every major decision should strengthen at least one of the following:

- Knowledge
- Trust
- Governance
- Scalability
- Sustainability

If a proposal weakens these principles, it should be redesigned or rejected.

---

# Organizational Philosophy

Knowledge is created once.

Knowledge is governed continuously.

Knowledge is reused infinitely.

Products are generated from knowledge.

AI accelerates work.

Humans remain accountable.

---

# Long-Term Goal

Build an enduring healthcare knowledge institution capable of serving millions of users while continuously improving through responsible AI, structured knowledge engineering, and expert human governance.

---

# Charter Review

This charter is intended to remain stable over many years.

Minor updates may occur as the organization matures.

Major revisions require executive approval and architectural review.
