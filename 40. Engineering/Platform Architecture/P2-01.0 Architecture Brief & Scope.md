# 1. Purpose

P2-01 defines the technical architecture of the Amar Smart Doctor Knowledge Platform.

The purpose of this milestone is to translate the approved:

- S1-04 Knowledge Foundation
- P2-00 Product & Business Architecture
- P2-00.1 Product & Business Architecture
- P2-00.2 Phase 1 Product Portfolio & Commercial Architecture

into a coherent, implementable technical architecture.

The architecture must establish the technical foundation required for the platform while preserving the governance principles established by the Knowledge Foundation.

The platform must be designed around governed medical knowledge rather than around the public website.

---

# 2. Architectural Principle

The central architectural principle is:

> **The Knowledge Platform is the system that governs and serves medical knowledge; products and experiences consume that knowledge.**

The architectural direction is therefore:

```text
Knowledge Foundation
        ↓
Product & Business Architecture
        ↓
Knowledge Platform Architecture
        ↓
Platform Implementation
        ↓
Products & Experiences
