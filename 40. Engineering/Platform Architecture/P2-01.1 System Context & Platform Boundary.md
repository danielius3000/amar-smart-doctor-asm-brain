# 1. Purpose

This document defines the system context and architectural boundary of the Amar Smart Doctor Knowledge Platform.

Its purpose is to establish:

- what the Knowledge Platform is
- what belongs inside the platform
- what remains outside the platform
- which systems and actors interact with the platform
- where the authoritative knowledge boundary exists
- how products consume platform capabilities
- how human and AI workforces interact with the platform

This document establishes the conceptual boundary before detailed technical architecture and technology decisions are made.

---

# 2. Architectural Context

Amar Smart Doctor is designed as an AI-native Healthcare Knowledge Platform.

The platform is built around governed medical knowledge rather than around a public website.

The architectural relationship is:

    Knowledge Foundation
            ↓
    Product & Business Architecture
            ↓
    Knowledge Platform Architecture
            ↓
    Platform Implementation
            ↓
    Products & Experiences

The Knowledge Platform is therefore the technical system that enables governed knowledge to be managed and consumed across multiple products and experiences.

---

# 3. Core Architectural Principle

The central principle is:

> **The Knowledge Platform is the system that governs and serves medical knowledge; products and experiences consume that knowledge.**

The public website is therefore a consumer of the platform.

It is not the authoritative source of medical knowledge.

AI systems are also consumers, assistants, or operators within governed workflows.

They are not the authoritative source of medical knowledge.

---

# 4. System Context

The Amar Smart Doctor ecosystem contains several major categories of actors and systems.

## 4.1 Human Users

Human users may interact with Amar Smart Doctor through:

- public website
- future mobile applications
- search experiences
- educational experiences
- appointment experiences
- future products

Human users consume product experiences rather than directly interacting with internal knowledge governance mechanisms.

---

## 4.2 Human Workforce

The human workforce may include:

- researchers
- editors
- knowledge engineers
- medical reviewers
- administrators
- product personnel
- other governed operational roles

The human workforce participates in knowledge operations and governance according to approved project standards.

---

## 4.3 AI Workforce

The future AI workforce may include specialized roles such as:

- Research AI
- Curation AI
- Knowledge Engineering AI
- Medical Reviewer AI
- Content AI
- Translation AI
- SEO AI
- Publishing AI
- Analytics AI

AI systems operate as governed participants in knowledge workflows.

AI-generated information must not become authoritative without appropriate verification and governance.

---

## 4.4 External Evidence and Information Sources

External sources may provide:

- medical evidence
- clinical guidelines
- research
- reference information
- other relevant authoritative material

These sources are inputs to the knowledge engineering process.

External sources do not automatically become authoritative Amar Smart Doctor Knowledge Assets.

---

## 4.5 Products and Experiences

The platform may serve:

- Amar Smart Doctor public website
- search experiences
- knowledge experiences
- discovery experiences
- appointment product
- future mobile applications
- future AI health experiences
- future education products
- future research products
- future enterprise products

Products consume approved platform capabilities.

# 6. Knowledge Platform Boundary

The Knowledge Platform includes the technical capabilities required to govern, manage, retrieve, and serve the governed Knowledge System.

The platform boundary conceptually includes:

## Knowledge Management

- Knowledge Assets
- medical concepts
- taxonomy
- evidence references
- relationships
- dependencies
- metadata

## Knowledge Governance

- review
- approval
- lifecycle management
- versioning
- traceability
- governance controls

## Platform Services

- knowledge retrieval
- search capabilities
- APIs
- authentication
- authorization
- integration services
- platform-level access control

The exact technical decomposition will be defined in later architecture work.

---

# 7. Systems Outside the Knowledge Platform

The following remain outside the core Knowledge Platform boundary unless a later architectural decision explicitly determines otherwise.

## 7.1 Public Website

The public website is a consumer of the Knowledge Platform.

It may render:

- knowledge pages
- disease/topic pages
- specialty pages
- doctor discovery
- search results
- other approved experiences

The website must not independently establish a competing source of medical truth.

---

## 7.2 Mobile Applications

Future mobile applications are product experiences consuming platform capabilities.

They are not part of the Knowledge Platform core.

---

## 7.3 Appointment Product

The appointment product is a Phase 1 product defined by the Product & Business Architecture.

It may integrate with the Knowledge Platform where appropriate.

The appointment product must not redefine the Knowledge System.

---

## 7.4 AI Models and External AI Services

AI models may be used by the platform or its workforce.

They remain tools or services rather than authoritative knowledge sources.

---

## 7.5 External Evidence Sources

External medical evidence sources remain external inputs.

They are not automatically part of the authoritative Amar Smart Doctor knowledge layer.

---

# 8. Authoritative Knowledge Boundary

The architecture must preserve the following conceptual flow:

    External Evidence
            ↓
    Evidence Intake
            ↓
    Validation / Curation
            ↓
    Knowledge Engineering
            ↓
    Human / Governance Review
            ↓
    Approved Knowledge
            ↓
    Product and Experience Outputs

The authoritative boundary is the governed and approved knowledge layer.

Derived outputs include:

- website pages
- articles
- search experiences
- AI responses
- educational content
- social content
- future product experiences

Derived outputs must not silently become authoritative medical knowledge.

---

# 9. Knowledge as the Platform's Core Asset

The platform must treat governed knowledge as the primary reusable asset.

The intended architectural model is:

    Governed Knowledge
            ↓
    Knowledge Platform
            ↓
    Reusable Capabilities
            ↓
    Multiple Products
            ↓
    Multiple Experiences

This prevents duplication of medical knowledge across products.

---

# 10. Product Boundary

Products are consumers of platform capabilities.

The conceptual relationship is:

    Knowledge Platform
            │
            ├── Knowledge Experience
            │
            ├── Search
            │
            ├── Discovery
            │
            ├── Appointment
            │
            ├── Future Mobile
            │
            ├── Future AI Experience
            │
            └── Future Products

Individual products may consume different subsets of platform capabilities.

Product-specific functionality must remain within appropriate product boundaries.

---

# 11. Human Workforce Boundary

Human workforce members operate through governed workflows.

The architecture must support appropriate separation between:

- research
- knowledge engineering
- medical review
- approval
- publishing
- administration

Human participation does not eliminate the need for system-level governance controls.

Where appropriate, governance must be enforced technically rather than relying solely on human discipline.

---

# 12. AI Workforce Boundary

AI systems may assist with:

- research
- evidence discovery
- extraction
- structuring
- generation
- translation
- SEO
- analysis
- automation

AI systems must operate within defined governance boundaries.

AI must not:

- silently bypass approval workflows
- independently establish authoritative medical truth
- overwrite governed knowledge without authorization
- bypass evidence requirements
- bypass lifecycle controls

The detailed AI architecture belongs to P2-04 but its boundary must be respected by the overall platform architecture.

---

# 13. Platform Boundary Rules

The following rules apply.

## Rule 1 — Knowledge Authority

The governed Knowledge System remains the authoritative source of medical knowledge.

## Rule 2 — Product Consumption

Products consume governed knowledge and platform capabilities.

## Rule 3 — No Duplicate Medical Truth

Products should not create competing authoritative knowledge repositories.

## Rule 4 — AI Subordination

AI remains subordinate to governance and appropriate human review.

## Rule 5 — Evidence Boundary

External evidence is an input to knowledge engineering and does not automatically become an approved Knowledge Asset.

## Rule 6 — Governance Enforcement

Critical governance controls should be enforceable through appropriate technical mechanisms.

## Rule 7 — Reusability

Platform capabilities should be reusable across multiple products.

## Rule 8 — Controlled Expansion

Future products may consume the platform without requiring the core Knowledge System to be duplicated.

# 15. Architectural Consequences

Establishing this boundary produces several important consequences.

### Consequence 1

The website cannot be treated as the core system of record for medical knowledge.

### Consequence 2

The Knowledge Platform must expose reusable capabilities to products.

### Consequence 3

The data architecture must support governed Knowledge Assets rather than simple article records.

### Consequence 4

Knowledge governance must be represented in the technical architecture.

### Consequence 5

AI integration must respect knowledge authority boundaries.

### Consequence 6

Future products can be added without duplicating the Knowledge System.

### Consequence 7

Platform architecture must remain simpler than the eventual ecosystem it may support.
