# Tool Responsibility Matrix

**Project:** Amar Smart Doctor

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Approved

**Owner:** CTO

---

# Purpose

This document defines the responsibilities of every human participant, AI system, and engineering platform involved in Amar Smart Doctor.

Its purpose is to eliminate ambiguity, prevent duplicated work, and establish clear ownership for every major engineering activity.

---

# Responsibility Principles

## Principle 1

Every responsibility has one primary owner.

---

## Principle 2

Supporting participants may assist but do not replace the owner.

---

## Principle 3

Final accountability always belongs to the human project owner.

# Tool Definitions

## Project Owner

Responsible for:

- Final business decisions
- Medical responsibility
- Legal responsibility
- Production approval
- Repository ownership

The Project Owner remains accountable for the platform.

---

## ChatGPT

Acts as the Strategic CTO.

Responsibilities include:

- Product architecture
- System architecture
- Documentation
- Engineering governance
- Technical reviews
- Sprint planning
- Risk assessment
- Long-term technical strategy

ChatGPT advises and reviews.

ChatGPT does not directly modify repositories.

---

## Google Antigravity

Acts as the Engineering Lead.

Responsibilities include:

- Software implementation
- Code refactoring
- Testing
- Automation
- Pull Request preparation
- Engineering execution

Antigravity implements approved plans.

---

## ASM Brain (Obsidian)

Purpose:

The human-readable engineering and knowledge system.

Stores:

- Vision
- Architecture
- Knowledge Bible
- ADRs
- Research
- Engineering documentation

ASM Brain is the canonical documentation workspace.

---

## GitHub

Purpose:

Version control and collaboration.

Stores:

- Source code
- Issues
- Pull Requests
- Releases
- CI/CD

---

# Conflict Resolution

If two tools appear to own the same responsibility:

1. Consult this matrix.
2. Follow the designated primary owner.
3. Escalate unresolved conflicts to the Project Owner.

---

# Future AI Roles

Additional AI systems should be introduced only when they have clearly defined responsibilities.

Examples:

- Medical Reviewer AI
- SEO Optimization AI
- Translation AI
- Analytics AI
- Customer Support AI

Every future AI must have:

- a defined purpose
- defined inputs
- defined outputs
- defined boundaries

---

# Review Policy

This matrix should be reviewed whenever:

- a new engineering tool is introduced
- AI responsibilities change
- engineering workflow changes
- governance documents are updated

---

# Closing Statement

Engineering organizations succeed when ownership is clear.

This responsibility matrix ensures that every participant understands their role and collaborates without unnecessary overlap.
