# P2-00.2 — Phase 1 Product Portfolio & Commercial Architecture

## Purpose

This document defines the Phase 1 product portfolio and commercial hierarchy of Amar Smart Doctor.

It translates the Product & Business Architecture into a practical commercial structure that identifies:

- what products exist,

- who they serve,

- how they create value,

- how they generate revenue,

- how products support one another,

- and which commercial models belong to Phase 1 versus future expansion.


The objective is to establish a commercially sustainable platform without compromising the healthcare knowledge, trust, evidence, and governance principles of ASM Brain.

---

# 1. Commercial Philosophy

Amar Smart Doctor will not depend on a single revenue source.

The platform will use a layered commercial architecture in which:

**Knowledge creates audience.**

**Audience creates discovery and intent.**

**Discovery creates transactions.**

**Provider participation creates recurring B2B revenue.**

**Scale creates additional monetization opportunities.**

The commercial architecture therefore follows:

```text
Trusted Knowledge
        ↓
Search Visibility
        ↓
Organic Audience
        ↓
Healthcare Discovery
        ↓
User Intent
        ↓
Transactions
        ↓
Revenue
```

At the same time:

```text
Amar Smart Doctor Audience
        ↓
Doctor Acquisition
        ↓
Appointment SaaS
        ↓
Provider Revenue
        ↓
Provider Retention
        ↓
Provider Growth
        ↓
Greater Healthcare Coverage
```

These two systems reinforce one another.

---

# 2. Phase 1 Product Portfolio

The Phase 1 portfolio consists of two primary product domains.

## Product Domain A — Healthcare Knowledge & Discovery Platform

The public Amar Smart Doctor platform.

Primary users:

- Patients

- Healthcare seekers

- Families

- General health information users

- People searching for doctors, hospitals, clinics, treatments, conditions, and healthcare services


Primary purpose:

- Provide trusted healthcare knowledge.

- Capture organic search demand.

- Help users understand healthcare topics.

- Help users discover healthcare providers and institutions.

- Generate high-intent healthcare journeys.

- Create the audience foundation for future products.


Primary commercial role:

**Audience acquisition and intent generation.**

---

## Product Domain B — Doctor Appointment SaaS

The existing appointment product operating at:

`appointment.amarsmartdoctor.com`

Primary customer:

**Doctor / Healthcare Provider**

Primary end user:

**Patient**

Product type:

**B2B2C Doctor Appointment SaaS**

Primary purpose:

- Provide doctors with an online appointment system.

- Provide patients with a convenient booking mechanism.

- Convert healthcare intent into an actionable appointment.

- Establish a recurring provider-side commercial relationship.


The appointment product is a first-class Phase 1 product.

---

# 3. Product Relationship

The two product domains should remain logically distinct but commercially connected.

```text
                  AMAR SMART DOCTOR
                         │
             ┌───────────┴───────────┐
             │                       │
             ▼                       ▼
      KNOWLEDGE &                 APPOINTMENT
       DISCOVERY                     SaaS
             │                       │
             │                       │
        Patient Side            Provider Side
             │                       │
             ▼                       ▼
        User Intent              Doctor Revenue
```

The knowledge platform should not become the appointment application's transactional database.

The appointment application should not become a parallel knowledge repository.

ASM Brain remains the governed knowledge operating system.

# 5. Level 1 — Healthcare Knowledge & Discovery

The public platform is primarily an audience acquisition engine during Phase 1.

Its most important objective is not immediate revenue.

Its primary objectives are:

1. Build trusted medical knowledge.

2. Build organic search visibility.

3. Capture healthcare search intent.

4. Build healthcare entity coverage.

5. Create patient discovery journeys.

6. Create provider discovery journeys.

7. Establish Amar Smart Doctor as a trusted healthcare information destination.


The platform should therefore optimize for:

- search demand,

- topical authority,

- entity authority,

- user satisfaction,

- trust,

- discoverability,

- repeat usage,

- conversion opportunities.


---

# 6. Level 2 — Consumer Advertising

Advertising is a legitimate Phase 1 monetization layer for eligible public content.

Potential mechanisms include:

- Google AdSense

- Other suitable advertising networks

- Future direct advertising partnerships


Advertising must remain subordinate to healthcare trust.

Advertising must not:

- determine medical authority,

- alter evidence status,

- influence medical recommendations,

- masquerade as medical content,

- compromise editorial independence,

- create misleading commercial endorsements.


The platform should maintain a clear distinction between:

```text
Medical Knowledge
```

and

```text
Commercial Advertising
```

Advertising is therefore:

**a monetization layer on top of audience acquisition, not a substitute for the knowledge product.**

---

# 7. Level 3 — Doctor Appointment SaaS

The Doctor Appointment SaaS is the first direct commercial product of Amar Smart Doctor.

Current product:

`appointment.amarsmartdoctor.com`

Current operating model:

- Doctor contacts Amar Smart Doctor.

- Account is created.

- Appointment system is configured.

- Doctor receives an appointment URL.

- Patients use the system to request/book appointments.


The current model is assisted/manual onboarding.

This is intentional during the early validation stage.

---

# 8. Current Appointment Commercial Model

The current commercial model consists of three components.

## 8.1 One-Time Setup Fee

The doctor pays a one-time setup/onboarding fee.

Purpose:

- account provisioning,

- configuration,

- appointment URL setup,

- initial implementation,

- onboarding support.


The exact amount is a commercial parameter and should not be permanently embedded into product architecture.

---

## 8.2 Per-Appointment Fee

The current model charges:

**৳10 per appointment**

This is the currently validated transaction-based pricing model.

It should be treated as current commercial configuration rather than an immutable architectural rule.

---

## 8.3 Monthly Platform Fee

A potential recurring subscription model has been identified:

**৳2,000/month**

This model is currently being validated with the first doctor customer.

It should therefore be classified as:

**Commercial Hypothesis / Validation Stage**

rather than fully validated recurring revenue.

The subscription may become the primary recurring revenue mechanism if customer value and retention justify it.

---

# 9. Current Provider Acquisition Model

At present, doctors do not automatically self-register.

The current acquisition flow is:

```text
Doctor Interest
      ↓
Contact Amar Smart Doctor
      ↓
Commercial Discussion
      ↓
Account Setup
      ↓
Appointment URL Creation
      ↓
Onboarding
      ↓
Live Appointment System
```

This manual process is appropriate at the current stage because the provider base is small.

The objective at this stage is learning rather than premature automation.

We need to validate:

- doctor demand,

- onboarding effort,

- pricing,

- appointment volume,

- retention,

- perceived value,

- support requirements,

- subscription willingness.


---

# 10. Provider Acquisition from the Main Platform

A major strategic objective is for the main Amar Smart Doctor platform to become a **doctor acquisition channel**.

The future provider acquisition journey should be:

```text
Doctor discovers Amar Smart Doctor
              ↓
Sees Appointment Platform
              ↓
Learns product benefits
              ↓
Views pricing / commercial model
              ↓
Requests appointment system
              ↓
Provider onboarding
              ↓
Appointment SaaS activation
```

Therefore the public platform will eventually require provider-facing commercial entry points such as:

- "For Doctors"

- "Doctor Appointment System"

- "Get Your Appointment Page"

- "Start Your Appointment System"

- provider landing pages,

- provider onboarding information,

- product demonstration,

- contact/request flow.


The exact implementation belongs to the relevant product and UX planning documents.

## Channel A — Direct Provider Acquisition

```text

  ↓
Appointment SaaS
```

The doctor independently discovers the product and becomes a customer.

---

## Channel B — Platform-Driven Provider Acquisition

```text

Amar Smart Doctor
  ↓

  ↓
Appointment Opportunity
  ↓
Appointment SaaS
```

The platform itself becomes the provider acquisition engine.

This channel becomes increasingly valuable as Amar Smart Doctor's search visibility and healthcare entity coverage grow.

# 12. B2B2C Business Model

The Appointment SaaS creates a B2B2C relationship.

```text
                  AMAR SMART DOCTOR
                         │
             ┌───────────┴───────────┐
             │                       │
          DOCTOR                  PATIENT
          (B2B)                    (B2C)
             │                       │
             ▼                       ▼
       Pays ASM                  Uses booking
             │                       │
             └──────────┬────────────┘
                        ▼
                  Appointment
                    Platform
```

The doctor is the commercial customer.

The patient is the end user.

This distinction must be preserved in future product, billing, analytics, and identity architecture.

# 17. Commercial Priority

The commercial priority for Phase 1 is:

### Priority 1

**Build the knowledge and search engine.**

Without audience and trust, the commercial layers remain weak.

### Priority 2

**Grow healthcare discovery.**

Build the entity and provider ecosystem.

### Priority 3

**Grow the Appointment SaaS.**

Convert providers into paying customers.

### Priority 4

**Validate recurring provider revenue.**

Determine whether the monthly platform subscription produces sustainable retention.

### Priority 5

**Monetize eligible consumer traffic.**

Introduce advertising carefully as traffic becomes meaningful.

### Priority 6

**Expand commercial products only after the core flywheels work.**

---

# 18. Commercial Principles

The following principles are approved.

### Principle 1 — Knowledge First

Revenue generation must never cause the platform to sacrifice medical knowledge quality.

### Principle 2 — Trust First

Commercial incentives must never override healthcare trust.

### Principle 3 — Multiple Revenue Streams

The platform should avoid dependence on one revenue mechanism.

### Principle 4 — Validate Before Scaling

Pricing and monetization hypotheses should be validated with real customers before being aggressively scaled.

### Principle 5 — Provider and Patient Separation

The provider is a commercial customer of the Appointment SaaS while the patient is its primary end user.

### Principle 6 — Organic and Paid Separation

Commercial participation must not silently determine organic authority or medical credibility.

### Principle 7 — Manual Before Automation

Early provider onboarding may remain manual until the process is sufficiently validated to justify automation.

### Principle 8 — Reusable Infrastructure

Commercial products should reuse the same governed healthcare knowledge and canonical entity architecture wherever appropriate.

# Decision / Conclusion

The Phase 1 commercial architecture establishes that:

1. Amar Smart Doctor's primary audience engine is healthcare knowledge, search, and discovery.

2. Consumer traffic may be monetized through appropriate advertising networks, including Google AdSense, without compromising healthcare trust.

3. The Doctor Appointment SaaS is the first direct commercial product.

4. The current Appointment SaaS model consists of a one-time setup fee and ৳10 per appointment.

5. A ৳2,000/month platform subscription is currently a commercial hypothesis being validated.

6. The Appointment SaaS is a B2B2C product.

7. Doctors are the commercial customers while patients are the primary end users.

8. The main Amar Smart Doctor platform should become a provider acquisition channel for the Appointment SaaS.

9. Early provider onboarding should remain assisted/manual until the commercial model is sufficiently validated.

10. Advertising and Appointment SaaS revenue serve different sides of the platform and should remain commercially distinguishable.

11. Provider premium services, institution monetization, B2B services, APIs, and future products are strategic expansion layers rather than immediate Phase 1 commitments.

12. Monetization must be designed from the beginning but scaled according to validated demand.

13. Commercial participation must never silently purchase medical authority, evidence status, or organic credibility.

14. The long-term business model is a dual-sided flywheel connecting healthcare users, providers, knowledge, search, appointments, and revenue.
