# P2-00.1 — Product & Business Architecture

## Purpose

This document defines the product and business architecture of Amar Smart Doctor.

It translates the organization's strategic vision and operating model into a clear product architecture that can guide product decisions, business development, knowledge architecture, and future engineering architecture.

This document exists to answer:

> **What are we building, why are we building it, how does it create user value, and how does it create sustainable business value?**

It is intentionally positioned before detailed product requirements and engineering architecture.

---

# Context

Amar Smart Doctor is being developed as an AI-native healthcare knowledge organization whose primary asset is structured, governed, and trustworthy medical knowledge.

The organization's operating model establishes:

- Knowledge as the primary asset.

- Products as consumers of knowledge.

- AI as an accelerator.

- Humans as accountable governors.

- Technology as an enabler of the operating model.


See:

- [[Amar Smart Doctor Operating Model v1.0]]

- [[Vision]]

- [[Mission]]

- [[Strategic Principles v1.0]]


The product architecture defined here extends those principles into the user-facing and commercial product ecosystem.

---

# 1. Product Vision

Amar Smart Doctor aims to become a highly trusted and highly discoverable healthcare knowledge and discovery platform, initially focused on Bangladesh and designed for long-term international scalability.

The platform will help people:

**Learn → Understand → Discover → Decide → Act**

across healthcare information and healthcare provider discovery.

The platform will be designed for both:

- English

- Bangla


from the beginning.

Search visibility is a core product requirement.

Amar Smart Doctor should be engineered so that relevant healthcare searches can discover the platform through:

- Google Search

- traditional search engines

- AI search and answer engines

- future healthcare discovery interfaces


The objective is not to guarantee rankings.

The objective is to build the strongest possible foundation for legitimate organic discoverability through high-quality knowledge, semantic structure, technical excellence, entity relationships, trust, and user value.

---

# 2. Core Product Principle

The fundamental product principle is:

> **Knowledge is the primary product asset.**

The public website is an output of the knowledge system.

Doctor discovery is an output of the knowledge and entity system.

Search visibility is an outcome of the quality and structure of the platform.

Future products should consume and reuse the same governed knowledge rather than creating independent competing knowledge stores.

Conceptually:

```text
Structured Knowledge
        ↓
Platform
        ↓
Products & Experiences
        ↓
User Value
        ↓
Business Value
```

This principle must remain intact as the organization scales.

Amar Smart Doctor will consist of interconnected product capabilities rather than one monolithic product.

```text
        ↓                       ↓                       ↓
```

These capabilities are connected but should not necessarily be implemented as one software system.

# 5. Search Engine Strategy

Search is a core acquisition mechanism.

The platform must be designed around search intent rather than treating SEO as a publishing-layer optimization added after development.

Search architecture must eventually account for:

- crawlability

- indexability

- URL architecture

- canonicalization

- internal linking

- structured data

- semantic relationships

- multilingual SEO

- page performance

- content quality

- topical authority

- entity authority

- freshness

- search intent

- technical accessibility


Search optimization must cover both:

### English

and

### Bangla

The platform should also account for common Bangla search behavior, spelling variations, terminology differences, and transliteration where appropriate.

---

# 6. AI Search Strategy

The platform should be designed to become a reliable source that AI-powered search and answer systems can discover, understand, and reference.

This requires:

- clearly defined entities

- structured relationships

- authoritative sources

- evidence-aware content

- transparent authorship and review

- stable URLs

- semantic clarity

- machine-readable information

- consistent terminology

- high-quality healthcare content


The objective is not to manipulate AI systems.

The objective is to make Amar Smart Doctor's information sufficiently clear, structured, trustworthy, and authoritative to be useful to both users and machine-mediated discovery systems.

---

# 7. Healthcare Discovery Engine

The Discovery Engine connects healthcare knowledge with real-world healthcare entities.

Potential entities include:

### Providers

- Doctors

- Specialists


### Institutions

- Hospitals

- Clinics

- Diagnostic Centers


### Healthcare concepts

- Specialties

- Treatments

- Procedures

- Conditions

- Services


### Geography

- Country

- Division

- District

- City

- Area


The system should understand relationships between these entities.

For example:

```text
Doctor
 ├── specializes in → Specialty
 ├── treats → Condition
 ├── performs → Procedure
 ├── works at → Hospital
 └── practices in → Location
```

This relationship-oriented architecture is essential for both useful discovery and scalable search architecture.

---

# 8. Doctor Appointment Product

The Amar Smart Doctor ecosystem already includes an operational Doctor Appointment product.

Current product location:

`appointment.amarsmartdoctor.com`

This product will be treated as a **first-class Phase 1 product**, not a future feature.

Its primary role is to convert high-intent healthcare discovery into action.

The intended relationship is:

```text
Search
  ↓
Knowledge / Discovery
  ↓
Doctor Profile
  ↓
Appointment Intent
  ↓
Appointment Product
  ↓
Booking
```

The appointment system should remain a logically distinct application because appointment operations have their own requirements, workflows, transactional data, and operational concerns.

The public Amar Smart Doctor platform should integrate with it through clearly defined interfaces.

---

# 9. Product Separation Principle

The appointment application should not be unnecessarily merged with ASM Brain.

ASM Brain is the knowledge operating system.

The appointment application is a transactional healthcare product.

Conceptually:

```text
                         ASM BRAIN
                            │
                            ↓
                   KNOWLEDGE PLATFORM
                            │
             ┌──────────────┼──────────────┐
             ↓              ↓              ↓
         Website        Discovery      Integrations
                                            │
                                            ↓
                                  Appointment Product
```

This separation protects maintainability while allowing the ecosystem to behave as one product experience.

---

# 10. Canonical Healthcare Identity

A core architectural requirement is the ability to maintain canonical identities for healthcare entities.

A doctor should remain the same underlying entity regardless of where the user encounters that doctor.

For example:

```text
Canonical Doctor ID
        │
        ├── Doctor Profile
        ├── Specialty Relationships
        ├── Hospital Relationships
        ├── Knowledge Relationships
        ├── Appointment Identity
        └── Analytics Identity
```

The exact identity architecture will be defined during engineering architecture.

The principle is established here:

> **The ecosystem must not create disconnected identities for the same healthcare entity.**

```text

      ↓
More Organic Traffic
      ↓
More Healthcare Discovery
      ↓
More User Intent
      ↓
```

while appointment and future commercial products provide the monetization layer.

## 13.4 Programmatic & Display Advertising

Programmatic and display advertising may become an audience-based monetization channel for Amar Smart Doctor.

This channel is particularly relevant for high-volume informational healthcare traffic where the primary user intent may be education rather than an immediate commercial transaction.

Potential mechanisms may include:

- Google AdSense

- Google Ad Manager, where appropriate at a later scale

- Other reputable advertising networks

- Direct advertising partnerships

- Clearly identified sponsored placements


The exact advertising technology will be selected later based on:

- traffic scale

- geographic audience

- advertiser demand

- healthcare advertising policies

- user experience

- SEO impact

- privacy requirements

- revenue performance


### Advertising Principle

Advertising monetizes audience attention.

It must not determine:

- medical authority

- evidence status

- editorial approval

- medical claims

- organic search ranking


Advertising must remain clearly distinguishable from medical information and recommendations.

Amar Smart Doctor may restrict or disable advertising on pages or contexts where advertising could negatively affect:

- medical trust

- user safety

- comprehension

- privacy

- page performance

- search quality

- brand credibility


Advertising is therefore an architectural monetization capability, **not a Phase 1 implementation commitment**.

## 13.6 Commercial Trust Boundary

Commercial participation must never silently purchase medical authority.

The platform must maintain a clear separation between:

```text
Medical Authority
       ≠
Organic Discovery
       ≠
Commercial Visibility
```

A provider's commercial relationship with Amar Smart Doctor must not automatically determine:

- medical authority

- evidence status

- editorial approval

- medical claims

- organic search ranking


Commercial products may provide legitimate additional functionality, visibility, or services within clearly defined commercial areas.

The distinction between medical information and commercial participation must remain transparent to users.

This boundary is a permanent architectural principle and must survive future monetization expansion.

# 14. Organic and Commercial Separation

A foundational business principle is:

> **Commercial participation must never silently purchase medical authority.**

Paid participation must not automatically determine:

- medical authority

- evidence status

- medical claims

- editorial approval

- organic search ranking


Commercial placements must be clearly distinguishable where appropriate.

Organic discovery should primarily reflect relevance, quality, trust, and user value.

This principle protects:

- users

- medical credibility

- search credibility

- long-term brand value

- regulatory and ethical integrity


---

# 15. Trust Architecture

Healthcare requires a stronger trust model than ordinary publishing platforms.

The system must distinguish between:

- medical knowledge

- evidence

- editorial interpretation

- provider information

- commercial information

- sponsored content

- advertisements

- AI-generated drafts

- human-reviewed content

- user-generated information


AI-generated information must not automatically become authoritative knowledge.

Medical information must follow the appropriate verification and governance processes defined by ASM Brain.

---

# 16. Bilingual Product Architecture

English and Bangla are first-class product languages.

The system should not assume that Bangla is simply an English translation.

Shared underlying knowledge may support both experiences while language-specific representations may require independent:

- titles

- descriptions

- terminology

- search intent

- metadata

- URL structures

- internal links

- structured data

- spelling variations

- transliteration handling


Conceptually:

```text
                  STRUCTURED KNOWLEDGE
                           │
                 ┌─────────┴─────────┐
                 ↓                   ↓
              ENGLISH              BANGLA
                 │                   │
            SEO EXPERIENCE      SEO EXPERIENCE
```

The knowledge foundation should be reusable without unnecessarily duplicating the underlying truth.

---

# 17. Phase 1 Strategic Scope

Phase 1 will establish the foundation for the long-term Amar Smart Doctor ecosystem.

The strategic priorities are:

1. Structured healthcare knowledge

2. English + Bangla

3. Search-first architecture

4. Google and AI-search discoverability

5. Healthcare entity discovery

6. Doctor and provider profiles

7. Hospital and clinic discovery

8. Integration with the existing Doctor Appointment product

9. Strong trust and evidence architecture

10. Foundational analytics and conversion measurement

11. Sustainable monetization architecture


The platform should prioritize these foundations before expanding into secondary products.

---

# 18. What Phase 1 Is Not

Phase 1 should not attempt to build every possible healthcare technology product.

The following should not automatically become Phase 1 scope:

- full hospital management systems

- electronic medical records

- clinical decision support

- complex telemedicine infrastructure

- broad patient management systems

- every possible monetization model

- international expansion infrastructure beyond what is reasonably required for future scalability


These may become future products.

The architecture should preserve the option to build them later without prematurely implementing them now.

```text
          Knowledge      Evidence    Governance
              │
              ↓
       PRODUCT PLATFORM
```

# 22. Strategic North Star

Amar Smart Doctor should become a healthcare platform where a person can move naturally from:

**Question → Knowledge → Understanding → Healthcare Discovery → Decision → Appointment**

while the organization continuously improves:

**Knowledge → Evidence → Governance → Trust**

The long-term strategic position is therefore:

> **A trusted, structured, bilingual healthcare knowledge and discovery platform with an integrated commercial healthcare action layer.**

---

# Decision / Conclusion

The following principles are approved as the foundation for subsequent product and engineering architecture:

1. Amar Smart Doctor is a healthcare knowledge and discovery platform.

2. Knowledge is the primary product asset.

3. Search visibility is a core product requirement.

4. English and Bangla are first-class product languages.

5. Google and AI-search discoverability are strategic objectives.

6. Healthcare entities must be represented as connected relationships.

7. The existing Doctor Appointment System is a first-class Phase 1 product.

8. The appointment product remains logically separate from ASM Brain.

9. Canonical healthcare identities must exist across the ecosystem.

10. Knowledge-driven traffic is the primary acquisition engine.

11. Appointment is the first major commercial conversion layer.

12. Monetization must be designed from the beginning but implemented progressively.

13. Organic discovery and commercial placement must remain distinguishable.

14. Healthcare trust and knowledge governance must not be compromised for monetization.

15. Future products must be able to reuse the same governed knowledge foundation.

16. Engineering architecture must follow these product and business requirements.
