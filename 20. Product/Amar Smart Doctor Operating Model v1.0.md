# Amar Smart Doctor Operating Model (ASDOM) v1.0

**Project:** Amar Smart Doctor

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Approved

**Owner:** Strategic CTO

---

# Purpose

This document defines how Amar Smart Doctor operates as an AI-native healthcare knowledge organization.

It is the highest-level description of the platform's operating model.

All future systems, products, engineering decisions, AI workflows, and knowledge assets must align with this model.

---

# Mission

Build the world's most trusted AI-native healthcare knowledge organization.

The organization exists to transform verified medical knowledge into trusted products that improve healthcare accessibility, education, and decision-making.

---

# Core Philosophy

Knowledge is the primary asset.

Structured knowledge is transformed into valuable products through AI-assisted workflows under human governance.

Technology serves knowledge.

AI accelerates knowledge.

Humans remain accountable.

# Organizational Layers

Amar Smart Doctor consists of five major layers.

## Layer 1 — Governance

Purpose:

Defines principles, policies, authority, and long-term direction.

Includes:

- Vision
- Constitution
- EOS
- ADRs
- Documentation Standards

---

## Layer 2 — Knowledge

Purpose:

Maintain structured medical knowledge.

Examples:

- Diseases
- Symptoms
- Medicines
- Doctors
- Hospitals
- Procedures
- Guidelines

Knowledge is independent from presentation.

---

## Layer 3 — AI Workforce

Purpose:

Transform structured knowledge into operational outputs.

Departments may include:

- Research
- Medical Review
- Content
- SEO
- Translation
- Publishing
- Analytics

Every AI role has defined responsibilities and boundaries.

---

## Layer 4 — Products

Purpose:

Deliver knowledge to users.

Products may include:

- Website
- Mobile Apps
- APIs
- Search Platform
- Professional Tools
- Educational Products

Products consume knowledge.

They do not own knowledge.

---

## Layer 5 — Infrastructure

Purpose:

Provide technical capabilities.

Examples:

- GitHub
- ASM Brain
- Google Antigravity
- Hosting
- Databases
- CI/CD
- Monitoring

Infrastructure enables every other layer.

# Human Governance

Humans are responsible for:

- Ethics
- Medical accountability
- Legal compliance
- Strategic direction
- Final approval

AI assists.

Humans govern.

---

# AI Governance

AI systems are specialists.

Every AI has:

- A defined purpose
- A defined owner
- Defined inputs
- Defined outputs
- Defined boundaries

AI collaboration must be documented.

No AI operates without governance.

---

# Engineering Principles

Engineering exists to support the operating model.

Software architecture follows organizational architecture.

Technology decisions should strengthen the operating model rather than dictate it.

---

# Future Expansion

The operating model is designed to support future products without changing its core principles.

Examples:

- International editions
- Additional languages
- Clinical decision support
- Medical education
- APIs
- Enterprise services

---

# Success Criteria

Amar Smart Doctor succeeds when:

- Knowledge quality continuously improves.
- AI workflows reduce repetitive work.
- Human experts focus on high-value decisions.
- Users trust the platform.
- The organization remains maintainable as it scales.

---

# Closing Statement

Amar Smart Doctor is not defined by its website, software stack, or AI models.

It is defined by its ability to transform trusted medical knowledge into valuable products through disciplined governance, responsible AI, and continuous improvement.
