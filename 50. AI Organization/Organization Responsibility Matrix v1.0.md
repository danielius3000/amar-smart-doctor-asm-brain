**Project:** Amar Smart Doctor

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Approved

**Owner:** Strategic CTO

---

# Purpose

This document defines the organizational responsibilities of every human role, AI department, AI worker, and supporting platform within Amar Smart Doctor.

The objective is to establish clear ownership, accountability, collaboration, and governance across the organization.

---

# Organizational Principles

1. Every responsibility has one primary owner.
2. Departments collaborate but do not duplicate ownership.
3. Humans remain accountable for strategic, medical, legal, and ethical decisions.
4. AI performs operational work within approved boundaries.
5. Every AI role must have a documented purpose and measurable output.
